Signals: what gold cannot say.

Golden Yellow is the brand accent, not a vocabulary. It cannot encode success, failure or a hyperlink, and it fails text contrast on every light surface. Five supporting hues carry meaning instead — each supplied in two audited variants, one for light surfaces and one for Onyx.

Standing rule: Golden Yellow is a surface, a fill and a signal shape — never body text, never a link, and never a foreground on white. On light surfaces gold reads at 1.63:1 against white, well under the 4.5:1 minimum. Use Onyx on gold, never gold on white.
